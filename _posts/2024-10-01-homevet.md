[HomeVet](https://homevetapp.com) is a cross-platform mobile application I co-founded and built from the ground up. It lets pet owners book in-home veterinary visits on iOS and Android.

Available on the App Store and Google Play at [homevetapp.com](https://homevetapp.com).

### What it does

Pet owners can browse available vets, book at-home appointments, manage their reservations, and receive real-time push notifications. The platform handles the full booking lifecycle from discovery to confirmation to completion.

### Tech Stack

- **Mobile:** React Native, Expo (iOS & Android)
- **Backend:** Supabase (PostgreSQL, Edge Functions, Auth, Storage)
- **Auth:** Supabase Auth (email + social login)
- **Notifications:** Expo Push Notifications

### What I built

As the sole developer and co-founder, I owned the full product end-to-end:

- Designed and built the entire booking flow from scratch
- Integrated Supabase for auth, database, file storage, and serverless edge functions
- Implemented user registration, reservation flows, and real-time push notifications
- Shipped to both Apple App Store and Google Play Store
